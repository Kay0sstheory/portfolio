Scribe

Private transcription that runs on my own Mac

Private
Problem
Interviews and voice notes sat in audio files I never went back to, and online transcription meant uploading private conversations.
Built
Drop an audio or video file on it and it writes out every word, entirely on my own Mac, with a word list that gets names and jargon right.
Result
A 53-minute interview became a full transcript, with who said what put back in.

How it works

  1. Drop an audio or video file onto the app
  2. It turns the sound into words on the Mac itself, nothing is uploaded
  3. A word list of names and terms keeps the spelling right
  4. The transcript is saved right next to the original file
