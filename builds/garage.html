Garage

A virtual showroom of my favourite cars

Private
Problem
I save cars I love all over Instagram, but never had one place to look at them properly or compare them.
Built
A dark showroom where every car sits on a turntable in 3D, with its size and space measured against an everyday Honda Civic.
Result
35 cars parked, each one I can spin, light and compare.

How it works

  1. Pick a car from its bay in the showroom
  2. Spin it on a turntable in 3D
  3. Set the lighting with presets and dials
  4. See its legroom and trunk measured against a Civic
