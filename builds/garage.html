What it does

The garage floor

  • Every car parked in its own bay, in a specific generation, colour and trim.
  • A lineup drawing shows every car side by side at true relative size.
  • One spec table compares every car at once.

Comparing fairly

  • Cabin and trunk space shown against one reference sedan, so every car is measured alike.
  • Outline overlays show exactly how much longer or taller a car is.
  • Official government data keeps space figures consistent.
  • Photo galleries filtered by colour or trim, with credits shown.

The 3D showroom

  • Drag to spin each car on a turntable in a glossy round hall.
  • Every car is scaled the same way, so size differences are real.
  • A lights panel with presets and fine controls, remembered between visits.

Adding cars fast

  • List many cars at once and pick photos and 3D models from numbered sheets.
  • Every 3D model is test-rendered and checked before it joins the garage.
  • Free-licence photos are fetched with their author and licence saved.

How it works

  1. Pick a car from its bay in the showroom
  2. Spin it on a turntable in 3D
  3. Set the lighting with presets and dials
  4. See its legroom and trunk measured against a Civic
