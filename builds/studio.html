What it does

Making a video

  • One command each to preview, render or package a video.
  • Preview stills are approved before any full render.
  • One command makes a website set: full video, short loop and poster.
  • Sizes for wide screens, phone portrait and 4K.

Smooth picture

  • Each frame is drawn on its own, so motion stays perfectly smooth.
  • Scripted screen takes with pointer, zoom, captions and click highlights.
  • Title and caption cards in any orientation.

Voice and sound

  • A local voice reads the script, then every line is checked by ear.
  • Lines that don't match the script are redone automatically.
  • Music sits lower under the voice, with free tracks and sound effects.

Built to reuse

  • Five ready visual styles, each a single file.
  • A playbook of hard-won lessons so mistakes aren't repeated.
  • Films only made-up copies of private apps, never real data.
