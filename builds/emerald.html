What it does

Goal map

  • A live mind map with life goals in lanes and progress on each.
  • Every project's latest status and open questions sit on the map.
  • Links show how one goal feeds another.

Every project at a glance

  • A card per project, fading as it goes untouched, with milestones and blockers.
  • One dated feed of milestones from every project.
  • Live to-do lists per project; a note and one click queue work for Claude.
  • Per-project mind maps grow by themselves so side ideas are never lost.

Task board and briefing

  • Projects post what they need from me to my Notion board, once each.
  • I drop tasks for Claude into a column and get them back done.
  • A nightly email sums up every project in three bullets.

Always on

  • Restarts itself after a reboot or crash.
  • Reachable from my Windows PC over a private network.
  • A health scan checks every project for risky or outdated parts.

How it works

  1. Every project keeps a short log of each work session
  2. Emerald watches those logs for changes
  3. Status and to-dos update the moment a file changes
  4. Projects that go quiet fade, so nothing is forgotten
