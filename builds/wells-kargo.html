What it does

Bringing the posts in

  • Downloads every saved post, with its video, photos and caption.
  • Turns every video's speech into text and reads the words shown on screen.
  • AI writes a short summary of each post and files it under a topic.
  • One hands-free overnight run fetches, reads, sorts and backs up new saves.

Browsing

  • An endless zoomable map of every post, grouped by topic, with real thumbnails.
  • A thumbnail wall sorted by date, creator or topic, plus a fold-out topic tree.
  • Works on my Mac, PC and phone, even with the storage drive unplugged.

Talking to the library

  • Ask for a plan and get steps and tools drawn from my own saves.
  • Every answer links back to the posts it came from.
  • Counting questions like "most common" are answered across the whole library.
  • An insights page shows taste-makers, interests over time and things to try.

Research while I build

  • Matches saved posts to features of my active projects, saying why each fits.
  • Flags helpful posts while I work on another project.
  • A quick note on any post becomes tags and a topic straight away.

How it works

  1. Instagram's export lists every saved post
  2. Each post is downloaded, transcribed and its on-screen text read
  3. An AI summary is kept beside my own notes, never mixed
  4. Posts are matched to the features of each project I'm building
  5. The right post surfaces when I start that feature
