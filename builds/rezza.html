What it does

Finding and scoring jobs

  • Scores any job posting out of 10 across five areas, with plain strengths and gaps.
  • Pulls in roles from LinkedIn, company career pages and job boards, scored in bulk.
  • Drops weak matches automatically and builds a tailored resume for every strong one.
  • Warns before I apply twice to the same role and checks the posting is still open.

Resume tailoring with a human in the loop

  • Suggests changes section by section, each with a written reason and alternative wordings.
  • I approve or reject every suggestion; only approved changes reach the resume.
  • Proof guard: it only uses resume points backed by a verified achievement card.
  • Every rebuilt resume passes a truth check before it can go out.
  • Live preview as I edit, then one click to a clean PDF and a Word copy.

Tracking every application

  • One board shows every role with a single clear state, from new to applied.
  • A day-by-day story for each application: scored, applied, every email and note.
  • Counters, filters and company search show where the hunt stands at a glance.

Inbox and networking

  • Reads job-hunt email, works out what each message means and updates the board.
  • When unsure, it asks me with a one-click question instead of guessing.
  • Keeps a contacts book of recruiters and referrals, tied to the roles they relate to.
  • Prepares interview folders with likely questions and how to talk about the resume.

How it works

  1. Paste a job posting
  2. The first model scores how well I fit the role
  3. The second rewrites the resume using only facts from my evidence bank
  4. The third checks every new line and blocks any it can't back up
  5. I review, approve and export
