Rezza

An AI job-application assistant.

Private
Problem
Tailoring a resume to every role took hours and invited exaggeration.
Built
Three AI models working in sequence. The app scores how well I fit a role, rewrites my resume from a bank of proven facts, and blocks any line it can't back up.
Result
A tailored, honest resume in minutes, with me approving every line.

How it works

  1. Paste a job posting
  2. The first model scores how well I fit the role
  3. The second rewrites the resume using only facts from my evidence bank
  4. The third checks every new line and blocks any it can't back up
  5. I review, approve and export
