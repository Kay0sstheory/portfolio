What it does

On the web

  • Compare up to five currencies side by side with live rates.
  • Never shows a blank: backup rate feeds take over if the main one fails.
  • Copy every conversion in one tap, ready for WhatsApp, Slack or email.
  • Create a branded picture of any conversion to share.
  • Dark and light themes, and a quick phone-friendly currency search.

Inside Claude and ChatGPT

  • Ask in plain words and a live Currensor card appears in the chat.
  • Change the amount on the card and every currency updates instantly.
  • No sign-in and no key needed, for anyone.

Private and open

  • Stores only anonymous daily counts, never who you are.
  • Free and open source.
  • Listed in the ChatGPT store and five public plugin directories.

How it works

  1. You type an amount, on the website or in a Claude or ChatGPT chat
  2. Currensor fetches live exchange rates
  3. The website shows every currency you follow at once
  4. In a chat, the plugin answers with a live conversion card
