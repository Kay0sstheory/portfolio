What it does

On the Mac

  • Hold or tap one key to talk; words appear at the cursor in any app.
  • Words type while I'm still speaking, so text keeps pace with my voice.
  • My own names and terms are favoured, so they stop coming out wrong.
  • Breaths and key clicks are ignored, so no phantom sentences appear.
  • Starts by itself and restarts if it ever stops.

On the phone

  • A real phone keyboard with a round voice pad and two thumb buttons.
  • Hold Speak to type; slide to Do for commands like new line or send.
  • Completely offline: the app cannot reach the internet at all.

Getting better over time

  • A read-aloud trainer collects clean samples of my voice.
  • Learns spelling fixes from recordings without breaking real English words.
  • One fair scorecard measures every change before it ships.
