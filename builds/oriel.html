Oriel

Prototype

A small window of peace for the Mac desktop: a calm scene that changes over time, with subtle, gentle motion.

  • Swift
  • AppKit
  • three.js
  • WebGL
530 x 230pixel window, weather-widget size
~0.9%of the computer's processor in use
7koi varieties in the pond prototype

What it does

On the desktop

  • A borderless window that sits on the wallpaper like a widget.
  • Drag it anywhere; its place is remembered.
  • Hidden behind app windows, so it never gets in the way.
  • Barely uses the computer, so it can run all day.

Simple controls

  • A menu-bar icon opens zoom, pan and speed controls.
  • Settings are remembered between launches.
  • Quit from the same panel, with nothing left running.

The koi pond prototype

  • Real sunlight patterns dance across a river-stone pond floor.
  • Seven koi varieties bend as they swim among lily pads and lotus.
  • A frog sits, hops and swims.
  • Motion slowed and eased on purpose, to feel calm rather than busy.
