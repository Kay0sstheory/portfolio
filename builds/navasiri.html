NavaSiri

A personal finance tracker.

Private
Problem
My bank statements showed where money went but never why.
Built
It reads statements from every account, sorts each spend into a category and learns from my corrections.
Result
One clear picture of our household spending, kept fully private.

How it works

  1. Drop in statements from every bank and card
  2. It reads every transaction and removes duplicates
  3. Each spend is sorted into a category
  4. My corrections teach it for next time
  5. One dashboard shows the whole household
