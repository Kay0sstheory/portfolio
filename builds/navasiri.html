What it does

Every account in one place

  • Reads statements from many Canadian and US banks, cards and brokers.
  • Re-importing a statement never double-counts a single row.
  • Checks each statement against the totals it prints for itself.
  • A coverage grid shows any missing month and why it is missing.

Sorting without guessing

  • Rules sort every transaction and learn from each correction I make.
  • Money moving between my own accounts is never counted as spending.
  • Refunds link back to the charge they reversed.
  • Bank alerts and receipts from email confirm who a payment really went to.

Seeing the whole picture

  • Spending this month against the three-month average, with clickable drill-downs.
  • Net worth, debts and investments, including gains and dividends.
  • Trips: booking emails prove a trip, then its spending is gathered automatically.

Planning ahead

  • Combine what-ifs like a pay change or a new loan in one run.
  • See the month a savings or debt goal will be reached.
  • Track an outside investment, like a property, with money in, out and net return.

Trust and safety

  • Health checks and a full audit trail of every import and change.
  • Automatic backup before every import, with one-click restore.
  • All data stays on my own computer, behind a password.

How it works

  1. Drop in statements from every bank and card
  2. It reads every transaction and removes duplicates
  3. Each spend is sorted into a category
  4. My corrections teach it for next time
  5. One dashboard shows the whole household
