About me

I'm a product owner by profession and a photographer by passion, based in Vancouver. For ten years I've worked between business and technology teams in retail, banking, health insurance and engineering consulting. Lately I've started building the products myself, with AI as my engineering partner.

Away from work I'm usually behind a camera, chasing how light plays on texture, form and nature, from city streets to the back roads of Vietnam. I'm a student of this world, always learning from the people who came before us and the people around us today. I love striking architecture, functional product design, food, training Muay Thai and a cold beer.
